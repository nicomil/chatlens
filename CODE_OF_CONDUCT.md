# Code of conduct

This is a small research tool with a small community. The standard is simple:
treat other people as colleagues.

Concretely — assume good faith, keep criticism about the code, and accept that
people come to this with different levels of experience in statistics, in
programming and in English. Someone asking a question that seems obvious is
someone the documentation failed.

Not acceptable: personal attacks, harassment of any kind, publishing anyone's
private information, or posting participant data in an issue.

Report a problem to formazione@aulab.it. Reports are handled privately, and the
maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions or block accounts where
that is the proportionate response.

This is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor
Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), kept short deliberately.
